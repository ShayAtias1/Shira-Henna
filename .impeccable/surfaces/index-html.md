# Surface: Shira's invitation page (index.html)

Scope: the single mobile-first page guests open from WhatsApp (intro → reveal → invitation → reply → success, plus returning state). Visitor mode: Experience, with one Operate task (RSVP in 10–15s).

Audience & job: invited women, personally addressed, on phones inside WhatsApp's browser; see the invitation, know when/where, answer. Proof/content: the real invitation artwork (flattened mockup), the real event facts. Constraints: see PRODUCT.md; Hebrew RTL, feminine first-person voice, reduced motion, pinch zoom.

Memorable moment: the blank back of the suite slides aside and settles toward the rear card, revealing the illustrated invitation; later the same Hamsa that crowns the reply sheet draws itself on success.

Unresolved: RSVP deadline, girls-in-count, host WhatsApp number, domain, layered artwork.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the invitation suite on silk, not a site around it. It refuses the hero-image-plus-form-card arrangement: the reply is a second sheet of the same stock whose top edge is the invitation's own die-cut crown.

OWN-WORLD: Blush silk ground made from the artwork itself (blurred), ivory/paper stock, dusty-rose double hairline rules, one antique-rose Hamsa, one botanical sprig. Bellefair for every spoken line and all figures; Assistant only for small labels. Deep-rose filled primary with an inset ivory hairline; hairline-framed secondary. No pills, cards, icons, gradients or badges.

STORY: Shira greets the guest by name → the guest opens the invitation → reads it (live date/venue line beneath) → answers on the reply sheet in one or two taps → gets a warm, designed confirmation with calendar/Waze as quiet afterthoughts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Intro: the blank die-cut cover card fills ~87% of width over the photographed suite; Hamsa at its crown, greeting in Bellefair ~34px at its middle, the deep-rose "לפתיחת ההזמנה" near its base; rear card and tassels visible around it. After reveal: the invitation card is the hero at the same scale, tassels dissolving into silk, the details line under them, and the reply sheet's ogee crown with its small Hamsa peeking at the fold.

FORM: The Ogee Sheet — position 7 on the ordered list of seven grounded structures (dealt lead), seed key a6671449. Signature interaction: card-stack reveal (~820ms); motion grammar: exponential ease-out, opacity/transform/clip, one sheen, one line-draw.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
